## 7. Construction progress animation (assessment only, not built)

**Usable photos already in the album:**
- **Willow-tree pergola deck (best).** Five shots from nearly the same spot: bare framing, decking down, steps framed with the pergola up, and finished. Copies are in `concepts/img/btl/`. The camera position shifts slightly between shots, so the house moves a little from stage to stage.
- **Blue house deck and stairs** (album photos around 1798–1813). Several stages exist, but the angles vary more.
- **Screened porch build** (around album 1907–1933). Framing through finished, with mixed angles.

**Where it fits:** the "Built to last, not just built fast" section, replacing the single photo. A working scroll demo using the willow deck is at `concepts/built-to-last-ideas.html` (Idea 3).

**Technical options:**
- **Image sequence tied to scroll (recommended).** This is how the demo works. It's light (5 to 8 photos, about 150 to 250KB each), smooth on phones, and works on iPhone Safari.
- **Higgsfield video, then frames.**
  - A generated video (5 to 8 seconds) can be cut into 40 to 60 still frames and scrubbed the same way.
  - It plays smoother, but the download is heavier (2 to 4MB as WebP frames), so it would load only when the section gets close.
  - Scrubbing an actual video file on scroll is unreliable on iPhone, so frames are the safer route.
- **On phones:** use a shorter scroll distance. When "reduce motion" is on, show only the finished photo.

**Assets needed from the client:**
- Two or more photos of one project taken from **the exact same spot** (ideally on a tripod or a marked spot), at each stage: site before, framing, decking or main structure, finished. Same time of day if possible.
- Full-resolution originals, not screenshots or texted copies.

AI tools like Higgsfield can invent details between photos. The result should be reviewed so it doesn't show work StoneBrook didn't do.
