## Design direction (from the inspiration sites)
- Leaned toward **builttowithstand.com**: Cinzel serif headings, generous whitespace, black/cream band rhythm, thin-outline buttons, eyebrow labels.
- Borrowed clarity and trust signals from **wolfcarpenters.com**: sticky header with phone and estimate button, a trust strip, and a clear process section.
- Warmed the black and white with **walkerwoodworking.com**-style stone and cream tones and one brass accent.
- Body text is 18px for the 50s–60s audience.
