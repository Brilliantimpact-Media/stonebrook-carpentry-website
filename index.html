A peek behind the walls.

Quality means doing it right where no one can see. Here's what that looks like on a real bathroom remodel.

  1. Step 1 of 3

    We open it up and show you.

    On this bathroom, we took the room down to the studs. Before anything new went in, we walked the homeowners through what we found and put the fix in writing.

  2. Step 2 of 3

    We do the part you'll never see.

    Every seam and corner of this shower was sealed with a waterproof membrane before a single stone went up. It's the step that keeps a shower lasting decades.

  3. Step 3 of 3

    Then we finish it beautifully.

    The result: a river stone shower with a corner seat, done right underneath and on the surface.
